YOUR BODY WAS BUILT TO LIVE BY LAWS THE MODERN WORLD HAS FORGOTTEN.

Wild animals don’t count calories. They don’t follow diet trends, track macros, or obsess over the latest health study. Yet in their natural environment, they instinctively eat, move, rest, and live according to patterns written into their design.

Human beings were designed with natural rhythms and needs too.

But modern life has changed the environment around us faster than our bodies can adapt. Highly processed foods, constant sitting, artificial light, disrupted sleep, chronic stress, and convenience at every turn have pulled us further and further from the conditions that once kept the human body naturally active, capable, and strong.

The Wild Plate explores 15 Natural Laws that reconnect modern life with the principles our bodies were designed to thrive on.

INSIDE, YOU’LL DISCOVER HOW TO:

  • Eat real foods that work with your body rather than against it
  • Build and preserve lean, functional muscle
  • Move more naturally — and with purpose
  • Work with your body’s signals for hunger, sleep, and recovery
  • Escape the modern habits that quietly undermine your health
  • Build a stronger, leaner lifestyle you can actually sustain

This isn’t another temporary diet. It’s a return to principles that were here long before diet culture existed.

THE WILD DOESN’T FOLLOW FADS.IT FOLLOWS LAWS.Maybe we should too.

Who it's for

This book is especially for adults over 40, people struggling with weight gain or low energy, fitness-minded readers, and anyone interested in natural health, nutrition, and how the created world reveals principles for human wellness.
